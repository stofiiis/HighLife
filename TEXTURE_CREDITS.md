# Texture Credits

Author of the mod: **stofiiis**

## HighLife V2 Item Set
- Art direction and final 16x16 pixel sprites: **stofiiis**.
- The source concept sheet is archived as `art_sources/highlife_texture_concept_v2.png`.
- Final item textures are generated deterministically by `tools/generate_item_textures.ps1`.
- Updated items: `alchemy_flask`, `dried_mystic_herb`, `herb_cookie`, `herb_roll`, `infusion_wand`, `mystic_herb_bundle`, and `mystic_herb_seeds`.

## HighLife V2 Block Set
- Art direction, final 16x16 pixel textures, and 3D block models: **stofiiis**.
- The source concept sheet is archived as `art_sources/highlife_block_concept_v2.png`.
- Final block textures and models are generated deterministically by `tools/generate_block_textures.ps1` and `tools/generate_block_models.ps1`.
- Updated blocks: `seed_mixer`, `drying_rack`, occupied drying rack, and all four `mystic_herb_crop` growth stages.
